## Starter reminders / 通用提醒

These are general questions, not the user's past mistakes / 不是用户过去的错误。

- Low P/E does not automatically mean cheap / 低 PE 不自动等于便宜。
- A cyclical rebound is not automatically long-term growth / 周期反弹不自动等于长期成长。
- Industry beta is not automatically company alpha / 行业 beta 不自动等于公司 alpha。
- Decompose growth into price, volume, costs and margins / 拆解价格、销量、成本和利润率。
- Match buy logic to sell or invalidation conditions / 买入逻辑对应卖出或证伪条件。
