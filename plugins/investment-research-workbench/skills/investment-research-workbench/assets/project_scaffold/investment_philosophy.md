## 2. My analytical framework / 我的分析框架

1. How does the company make money? / 公司靠什么赚钱？
2. What are the industry's core variables? / 行业核心变量是什么？
3. Is the thesis long-term growth, cyclical recovery, valuation repair, financial improvement, a short-term catalyst or mixed? / 长期成长、周期反弹、估值修复、财务改善、短期催化还是混合？
4. Are results driven by price, volume, cost, capacity, mix, the industry cycle or one-offs? / 业绩来自价格、销量、成本、产能、产品结构、行业周期还是一次性因素？
5. Is the moat real? / 护城河是否真实？
6. What expectations are priced into valuation? / 当前估值隐含什么预期？
7. What would invalidate the thesis? / thesis invalidation 是什么？
8. What are the buy, hold and sell conditions? / 买入、持有、卖出条件是什么？
